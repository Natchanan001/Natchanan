A web-based order management platform currently being developed **for my own preorder business**, where I import merchandise from China and manage customer orders.

Unlike my academic projects, this system is being built to support the **day-to-day operations of a real business**. It was inspired by the operational challenges I face as a small business owner—tracking customer orders, managing purchases across multiple Chinese marketplaces, and providing transparent shipment updates without relying on spreadsheets or manual messaging.

The goal is to create a centralized platform that improves operational efficiency while allowing customers to independently check the status of their orders at any time.

- 🔍 Search & filtering
- 🔐 Authentication & Role-Based Access Control
